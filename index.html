Existing routes: /register/, /whatsapp/send-message, and /whatsapp/send-pdf. If your frontend and API use different origins, enable the frontend origin in your server's CORS configuration.

Your first message, in minutes.

One simple workspace for the conversations that matter.

  1. Connect your service

    Start your backend. Check the API base URL in Settings; it defaults to http://127.0.0.1:8000.

  2. Register your number

    Use “Register a number” to submit your phone number. Complete any further authentication your backend requires.

  3. Create something worth sending

    Add a recipient, write your message or attach a PDF, and optionally choose a future date and time.

  4. Follow your requests

    Activity shows responses to requests made on this page. Delivery tracking and a persistent scheduling queue require additional backend APIs.
